A slab leak is a leak in a pipe that runs under or inside a concrete foundation. The water cannot be seen, but it shows up as warm spots, a running water sound, a high bill or wet flooring. The 24/7 line is open now.

Be honest about the numbers. No agency publishes slab leak counts for Santa Monica, so this page cannot say how often they happen. They affect slab-on-grade homes and ground-floor units, and they are one cause among many of wet floors here.

Where slab leaks show up locally

Slab foundations are one of several foundation types in a city with housing this varied. The Census Bureau American Community Survey for 2019 to 2023 counts 46,457 households, with 28.8 percent owner occupied, so most ground-floor slab leaks affect rented units where the owner controls the plumbing.

The city also lies near an active fault. The 2025 Local Hazard Mitigation Plan update, as reported by the Santa Monica Daily Press, treats the Santa Monica fault as the most severe earthquake risk. Ground movement stresses rigid lines in a slab, and a line that has cracked can leak for a long time before anyone knows.

Safety steps for the first hour

Water, power and gas

If water is rising near outlets, stay out of it. If you smell gas, leave and call 911, because a slab can hide both water and gas lines. Close the main water valve if you can reach it.

  • Check the water meter with every fixture off. If it moves, water is leaking.
  • Close the main water valve.
  • Move belongings off wet floors.
  • Photograph wet areas and meter readings with dates.

How slab leak damage is handled

  1. 1

    Confirm the source

    A plumber with leak detection equipment locates the break. The restoration crew does not cut slabs or repair lines.

  2. 2

    Extract

    Pumps and wet vacuums remove water from floors, and drying mats lift moisture from hard floors.

  3. 3

    Dry

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the walls, flooring and baseboards. Readings are logged.

  4. 4

    Rebuild

    Wet baseboards and flooring are replaced once dry. See water damage repair.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for drying and water categories. A slab leak starts with clean supply water, and the city says up to 75 percent of local water is groundwater treated at its Arcadia plant.

What changes the cost

Time unnoticed

A leak that ran for weeks soaks more material.

Flooring type

Hardwood, laminate and carpet respond differently to water from below.

Rooms affected

Slab water spreads through baseboards and under walls.

Drying days

Concrete holds moisture, so drying can take longer.

Policies differ on slab leaks. Many cover the resulting water damage if the leak was sudden, and many exclude the cost of reaching and repairing the line. Read your policy or ask your agent, and report the loss promptly.

Find a service by storm or structure

Storm water, groundwater and lower-level flooding are handled under flood damage restoration. Roof and ceiling leaks after rain connect to roof leak water damage and ceiling water damage repair. Drying the building afterward follows the steps in structural drying.

How the services fit together

A typical loss moves through three stages. Extraction removes the bulk water. Mitigation dries the structure and logs moisture readings. Repair rebuilds whatever had to be removed. Cleanup and sanitizing sit between those stages when the water is dirty, and mold remediation joins the sequence when moisture sat long enough for growth.

Skipping a stage is the usual cause of repeat problems. Repair over wet framing invites mold. Drying without cleanup leaves contaminated materials in the walls. A good scope names each stage, who does it and how it will be confirmed.

Services in the order most calls need them

Emergency calls most often begin with emergency water removal and move straight into water mitigation. When water was dirty, water damage cleanup follows, and flood damage restoration applies when the source was storm water or sewage.

After drying, water damage repair brings the room back. Mold remediation is a secondary service. It matters when you find growth or smell mold after a leak, and it is also the first call when a bathroom or closet has had a slow, hidden leak.

What this line does not do

Fire and smoke cleanup are not part of this site. Basement waterproofing and foundation work are different trades, and if your real problem is that water keeps coming in from outside, dispatch will say so and point you toward a drainage or waterproofing contractor. The line does not offer equipment rentals.

The site does not publish exact prices. Prices depend on the water category, the area, the materials and the drying time, and a real number requires someone to see the damage. Call the line, and the scope and the way the price is set are explained before work starts.

The order a typical loss follows

Most losses move through the same order. Water is extracted first, wet materials are dried and removed if they cannot be saved, surfaces are cleaned, and the structure is repaired. Mold work enters the sequence only when growth is present or when materials stayed wet for too long.

Each service page explains one stage in detail. You do not need to know which stage you are in to call. Dispatch listens to the description and tells you what the situation sounds like.

Choosing between similar services

Emergency water removal and water mitigation overlap. Removal is about getting standing water out. Mitigation is the larger effort to stop the damage from spreading, which includes drying, monitoring and removing materials that cannot be saved.

Water damage cleanup is the handling of the affected surfaces and contents. Water damage repair is the rebuild. Flood damage restoration applies when water arrives from outside or from a sewer line, which changes the safety steps.

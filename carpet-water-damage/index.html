Carpet water damage restoration decides what to save and what to remove. Carpet and pad act like a sponge, holding water against the subfloor. Whether they can be dried in place depends on how clean the water was and how long it sat.

Lift furniture off the carpet, remove foil or wood blocks from the legs and keep people off the wet area. Then call the line and say where the water came from.

Save the carpet or replace it

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference crews use to classify water and decide what can be dried. Clean water from a supply line can often be extracted and the carpet dried in place if it is acted on quickly. Gray water and sewage change the answer, because carpet and pad that soaked in contaminated water are usually removed.

The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, and carpet that stays wet longer is more likely to be replaced. A synthetic carpet over a thick pad can hold water for days, even when the top feels dry.

Why carpet is common in Santa Monica water losses

The Census Bureau American Community Survey for 2019 to 2023 counts 46,457 households, with 1.92 people per household on average and only 28.8 percent living in a home they own. Rented apartments commonly have wall-to-wall carpet in bedrooms and living rooms, so a leak from a unit above often lands on carpet.

NOAA climate normals for the Santa Monica Pier station show most rain falls from November to March, so storm-driven leaks and open-door flooding cluster in those months.

Safety steps for wet carpet

Power cords and slip risk

Do not use a household extension cord or plug in a device on wet carpet. Wet carpet over concrete or tile is slippery. If the water came from outside or from a drain, keep children and pets off the carpet and wash your hands after contact.

  • Lift furniture and move belongings to a dry room.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet.
  • Open windows if the weather is dry.
  • Photograph the carpet and the label if it is visible.

How the crew handles carpet

  1. 1

    Extract

    A truck-mounted or portable extractor pulls water from the carpet and pad.

  2. 2

    Test and decide

    A moisture meter checks the pad, tack strip and subfloor, and the water category sets what is saved.

  3. 3

    Dry

    Air movers lift the carpet and dehumidifiers dry the room. Readings are logged.

  4. 4

    Replace or reset

    Pad is replaced and carpet is cleaned and restretched, or the full carpet is replaced.

For a larger loss, water mitigation covers the drying plan and water damage cleanup covers removal of ruined materials.

What changes the cost

Water category

Clean water costs less to handle than contaminated water.

Area

Square feet of carpet and number of rooms.

Time wet

Several days of dampness can end the chance of saving the pad.

Replacement

Carpet grade and pad thickness affect rebuild work.

Policies often cover sudden water damage to carpet and often exclude damage from a leak that was ignored. The California Department of Insurance advises reporting promptly and keeping records. In a rental, carpet may belong to the owner, so notify the manager.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Walking on wet carpet and pushing water deeper.
  • Leaving a wet pad in place and drying only the top.
  • Running a heater over wet carpet.
  • Reinstalling carpet over a damp subfloor.

If a musty smell appears, see mold remediation before laying new carpet.

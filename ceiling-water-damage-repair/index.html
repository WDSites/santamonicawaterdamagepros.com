Ceiling water damage repair replaces or restores the ceiling after water has soaked it. It comes last, after the source is fixed and the cavity above is dry. Repair before drying locks moisture in.

A brown ring, a bubble in the paint or a soft sag all mean water is or was in the ceiling. Which one it is decides the work. A dry old stain may need only a sealer and paint, and a wet sagging ceiling needs drying and removal first.

Where ceiling water comes from in Santa Monica

Most ceiling leaks in this city come from above, and what is above depends on the building. The Census Bureau American Community Survey for 2019 to 2023 puts owner occupancy at 28.8 percent, so most households live in rented homes, many of them in multi-unit buildings. A ceiling in one unit is often a floor in another.

That makes the source a question of who owns the pipe or the roof. A tub overflow, a failed supply line or a leaking drain in the unit above can soak a ceiling below. Roof leaks, air conditioning lines and failed flashing are the other common causes.

Safety before you touch a wet ceiling

Stay out from under a bulging ceiling

Wet drywall is heavy. A bulge can release water and fall. Keep people and pets out of the room, and leave the ceiling alone until a crew tells you how to release the water safely.

Turn off the light fixture and fan on that circuit if water is near them. Move furniture from below, lay plastic over what cannot move and photograph the ceiling with a date for your records.

How a ceiling repair is done

  1. 1

    Check moisture

    A moisture meter and thermal imaging camera confirm whether the ceiling and the cavity are dry.

  2. 2

    Remove damaged board

    Wet or sagging drywall is cut out past the damaged area, along with wet insulation.

  3. 3

    Hang, tape and texture

    New drywall is hung, taped and textured to match the surrounding ceiling.

  4. 4

    Seal and paint

    A stain-blocking primer goes on before the finish coat, which runs corner to corner so the patch does not show.

If the ceiling is wet when you call, the crew may start with water mitigation to dry the cavity, then move to repair. ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for how crews decide whether wet materials are dried or removed.

What changes the cost

Area

A single patch costs less effort than a full ceiling.

Texture

Popcorn, knockdown and orange peel each need matching.

Insulation and framing

Wet insulation, or damaged framing, adds work above the ceiling.

Paint

A full-ceiling coat avoids a visible patch and adds paint labor.

Insurance often covers a ceiling damaged by a sudden leak and often does not cover a slow leak that went unrepaired. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours, and a delay can turn a stain into a mold question.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Painting over a stain that is still damp.
  • Poking a bulging ceiling from directly below.
  • Repairing before the leak above is fixed.
  • Using only a white sealer on a ceiling that may have growth.

If the stain shows dark growth, see mold remediation before you scrub. For the full rebuild process, see water damage repair.

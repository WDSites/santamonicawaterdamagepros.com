A water heater leak ranges from a slow drip at a fitting to a split tank that releases dozens of gallons at once. Either one needs the water stopped and the surrounding materials dried.

Switch off the power or gas control to the heater, close the cold water valve above it and call the line. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 first.

Why water heaters fail and where the water goes

Tank heaters corrode from the inside, and the failure point is often the bottom seam or a fitting that has been weeping for months. Water heaters in Santa Monica sit in garages, closets, utility rooms and, in apartments, small cabinets that share a wall with a living space.

A leak in a closet runs under the door into the hallway and the floor beneath. In a garage it reaches stored boxes and the base of drywall. In a multi-unit building it can pass through the floor into the unit below.

California law adds another factor. Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion. A heater that has shifted, or a bracing strap that has rubbed a pipe, can start a leak, and a strong quake can break the connections.

Safety steps for the first hour

Power and gas first

For an electric heater, switch off its breaker only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. For a gas heater, turn the gas control to off. If you smell gas, do not touch switches. Leave and call 911. Hot water can also scald, so keep your hands away from a spraying line.

  • Close the cold water supply valve above the heater.
  • Move boxes, rugs and stored items off the floor.
  • Photograph the heater, the label and the water.
  • Keep the tank in place for the plumber and your insurer to inspect.

How the crew handles the leak

  1. 1

    Extract

    Submersible pumps and a wet vacuum remove water from the floor and nearby rooms.

  2. 2

    Find hidden water

    Moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera check the wall bases, floors and any room below.

  3. 3

    Dry

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run until the readings are dry, and baseboards or drywall are opened if needed.

  4. 4

    Clean

    Surfaces are cleaned, and ruined materials are removed. A plumber replaces the heater.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for how these steps are measured. Water that comes from a clean supply line is classed as Category 1 when it first escapes. The city reports that up to 75 percent of local water is groundwater treated at its Arcadia plant, so a new leak starts with treated supply water.

Mistakes to avoid and ways to prevent the next one

  • Leaving the heater on while the tank is leaking.
  • Throwing the tank away before your insurer can see it.
  • Skipping a drain pan under a heater above living space.
  • Ignoring a weeping valve or rust around the base.

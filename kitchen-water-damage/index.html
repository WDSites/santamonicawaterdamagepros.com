A kitchen is one of the hardest rooms to dry. Cabinets sit on toe kicks that trap water, appliances block access and flooring often runs under the cabinets. Kitchen water damage restoration is about reaching those hidden places before they swell or grow mold.

Close the valve under the sink or the appliance valve, lift items off the floor and call. Do not push water under the cabinets with a mop.

What is different about kitchen water

Kitchens combine supply lines, drains, a dishwasher, often a refrigerator ice maker and a sink with a garbage disposal. Any of these can leak, and the water follows the floor to the lowest point, which is often under a cabinet base. The Census Bureau American Community Survey for 2019 to 2023 puts owner occupancy in Santa Monica at 28.8 percent, so many kitchens belong to rented homes where the owner controls the plumbing.

For causes tied to a specific machine, see appliance leak water damage cleanup. This page covers the room: cabinets, counters, flooring, subfloor and walls.

Safety steps for a wet kitchen

Water and kitchen electricity

Outlets above counters, a refrigerator, a dishwasher and a range all use power. Stay out of water near them. Unplug appliances only if the outlet and floor are dry, and switch off the circuit at the panel if water is near wiring. Gas ranges need extra care, so leave if you smell gas.

  • Close the sink or appliance valve.
  • Move food, small appliances and cookware off the floor.
  • Photograph the cabinets, floor and walls.
  • Do not reconnect appliances until the crew says the area is safe.

How the crew dries a kitchen

  1. 1

    Open the hidden spaces

    Toe kicks are removed and a moisture meter checks cabinet bases, walls and subfloor.

  2. 2

    Extract and dry

    Wet vacuums remove water, then air movers, drying mats and dehumidifiers run.

  3. 3

    Remove what cannot dry

    Swollen particleboard, laminate and soaked drywall are removed.

  4. 4

    Rebuild

    Cabinets, flooring and walls are replaced once the readings are dry. See water damage repair.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for these steps. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, and cabinet bases are a classic place for growth to start unseen. The city says up to 75 percent of local supply is groundwater treated at its Arcadia plant, so most kitchen supply leaks begin as treated water.

What changes the cost

Cabinet material

Solid wood can sometimes be saved, particleboard often cannot.

Flooring

Tile, vinyl, laminate and wood respond differently.

Area under cabinets

Water under fixed cabinets takes more work to reach.

Drying days

Subfloor and wall cavities set the equipment time.

A sudden kitchen leak is often covered, and a long-running drip that ruined cabinets often is not. The California Department of Insurance advises reporting a loss promptly and keeping records. Keep damaged cabinet parts until your insurer has seen them.

Mistakes to avoid and prevention

  • Laying new flooring over a wet subfloor.
  • Leaving the toe kick in place and hoping it dries.
  • Using the dishwasher while the drain is leaking.
  • Ignoring a damp smell under the sink.

Check under the sink each month, replace old braided hoses and test the shut-off valves. If the room stayed wet for several days, see mold remediation.

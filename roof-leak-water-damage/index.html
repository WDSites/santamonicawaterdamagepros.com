A roof leak is water damage that starts from the top down. Rain finds a failed seal or lifted flashing, soaks insulation and drywall, and shows up as a stain or a drip. The 24/7 line is open now.

Place a bucket under the drip, move furniture and electronics away, and call. If the ceiling sags or bulges with water, keep people out from under it. A roofer fixes the roof, and the restoration crew handles the water inside.

How rain gets in on this coast

Santa Monica is dry for much of the year, and that hides roof problems. NOAA climate normals for the Santa Monica Pier station show about 13 inches of rain in a typical year, nearly all of it from November through March, with February the wettest month. Summer months are almost dry.

That pattern means a seal that failed in a dry spring often goes unnoticed until the first winter storm. Wind-driven rain near the ocean pushes water sideways under flashing, around skylights and through cracked vent boots. Flat roofs on apartment buildings pond water and strain every seam.

Safety steps during an active leak

Do not climb onto a wet roof

A wet roof is a fall risk, and roofers use harnesses for a reason. Stay inside. If water is dripping near a light fixture or ceiling fan, switch off the circuit at the panel only if it is dry and safe to reach.

  • Move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the drip zone.
  • Set a bucket or a trash can liner under the drip.
  • If the ceiling bulges, puncture it only if a crew tells you how. A small hole can release a lot of water.
  • Photograph the leak and the stain with a date.

The storm of January 2005 shows how fast the coast changes. According to the Santa Monica Daily Press, all four city fire stations handed out sandbags and the California Incline was closed for mudslide and flood risk. Roofs across the city were tested the same week.

How the crew handles the water inside

  1. 1

    Find the wet area

    A moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera map the ceiling, walls and attic edges that soaked.

  2. 2

    Remove what cannot dry

    Saturated insulation and sagging drywall are removed to stop water from spreading.

  3. 3

    Dry the cavity

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run, and readings are logged until the framing reaches its drying goal.

  4. 4

    Hand off to repair

    Once dry, water damage repair restores the ceiling and paint.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for how restoration crews measure and dry water damage. Rain through a roof is usually treated as clean or slightly dirty water at first, and it turns dirtier as it passes through attic insulation and dust.

What changes the cost

Size of the wet area

Square footage of ceiling, wall and floor reached.

Insulation and finishes

Soaked insulation and textured ceilings need more work than plain drywall.

Time wet

Water that sat for days costs more to dry than water caught the same day.

Access

Attics and second floors can slow equipment setup.

Mistakes to avoid and how to prevent the next leak

  • Painting over a stain before the cause is found.
  • Patching the ceiling while the insulation above is still wet.
  • Skipping a roof inspection before the first winter storm.
  • Letting leaves and debris collect around vents, valleys and drains.

After the roof is repaired and the structure is dry, see mold remediation if dark spots or a musty smell appear in the ceiling.

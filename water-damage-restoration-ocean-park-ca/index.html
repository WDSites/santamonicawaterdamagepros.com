Water Damage Restoration in Ocean Park, Santa Monica, CA

Cottages, apartment buildings and beach-side homes fill the southwest corner of Santa Monica. This page explains how water damage is handled there and what to do in the first hour.

Ocean Park fills the southwest corner of Santa Monica, from the beach inland to about Lincoln Boulevard and from Pico Boulevard south toward the city limit. Water damage here usually begins with a plumbing or appliance failure inside a building, and this page explains how the mix of homes shapes the response.

The boundaries are informal. Neighborhood reference descriptions place Ocean Park between Pico Boulevard and the southern city limit, and from the ocean east to Lincoln Boulevard. The City of Santa Monica recognizes the Ocean Park Association as a neighborhood organization, which supports the name being in everyday use. Streets are used below as approximate landmarks, not as official lines.

A beach resort that became a neighborhood

Developer Abbot Kinney and his partners laid out Ocean Park in the 1890s as a seaside resort and residential tract, with a pier and amusements. Local history reporting describes Main Street in later decades as the neighborhood hangout, lined with shops, bars and restaurants. Main Street is still the address most people know.

Reference descriptions of the housing mention early twentieth century Craftsman and Victorian houses, mid-century apartment complexes and newer high-end homes. That mix changes the response. A single cottage and a stacked apartment building lose water differently, and each needs a different first phone call.

A local historian quoted in the Santa Monica Daily Press describes projects near Main Street that put two levels of affordable housing above two levels of parking. In any stacked building, water from an upper unit can reach the unit below, so tell the building manager first and ask neighbors to check their ceilings.

What the record does and does not show about flooding

No public record reviewed shows that Ocean Park floods more often than other parts of Santa Monica. After a January 2005 storm, city officials said the drainage system and the slope of the city from the northeast corner down to the southwest limited damage. Ocean Park sits at that southwest end. That describes the lay of the land and is not a record of repeated flooding here.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified for this page. To check one address, use the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask your insurer, because standard homeowner policies usually exclude flood. Most restoration calls, here and elsewhere, start inside the building with a supply line, a water heater, a washing machine hose or a roof leak after rain.

Safety first: the first hour

Stay out of standing water if outlets, cords or the panel could be wet, and leave and call 911 if you smell gas or see sparks. Keep people and pets away from water that came from a drain or toilet. Close the water shutoff only when you can reach it safely, and photograph the damage from a dry spot.

  • Tell the building manager at once if you share walls or floors with other units.
  • Lift belongings that are safe to lift off wet floors and furniture legs off wet carpet.
  • Do not run a household vacuum or fans to deal with water from a drain. Contaminated water needs professional handling.
  • Call the line and say which cross streets you are near and where the water started.

How extraction and drying work

Crews begin with extraction, using pumps or wet vacuums to pull standing water out. Moisture meters and thermal imaging then locate water that has moved into walls and under floors. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the building, and readings are logged each visit until they match a dry reference. See water mitigation and structural drying for the equipment and the daily checks.

The cause decides the plan. A split line is covered under burst pipe water damage, and water from a drain is handled as contaminated under sewage backup cleanup. Not every material can be saved, and the crew tells you which ones can before work starts.

Insurance documentation

Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope can help describe what happened and what work is proposed. Whether the loss is covered depends on the cause and on your policy terms, so read the policy and ask your insurer. This site cannot tell you whether a claim will be approved.

Questions

Ocean Park water damage questions

Call the number on this page and give your cross streets. Dispatch will tell you whether a crew can reach you and what to do while you wait. Describe where the water started, because that decides the first step.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status is not verified. Only part of a neighborhood can fall inside a mapped zone, and Zone X does not mean flooding cannot happen. Check your own address on the FEMA map and ask your insurer about flood coverage.

Call your building manager or landlord so the source can be found and stopped. Then call the restoration line for the water that reached your unit. Photograph the damage and keep dated messages.

Ask your building manager or the City of Santa Monica Public Works whether the blockage is in the public sewer or in your own line. That tells you who repairs it. The cleanup of the affected rooms is a separate job.

Fans move air, but they do not find moisture inside walls or under floors. For clean water from a small spill, careful drying may be enough. For water from a drain or a large leak, call a professional.
